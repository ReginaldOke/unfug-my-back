Privacy Policy

Back Story · Last updated 1 November 2026

Back Story is a diary for back pain. It is made by Reginald Oke. It is built so that your diary stays yours: it lives on your phone, and we never see it.

What the app stores

Your check-ins (pain, stress, sleep, where it hurts), the things you log alongside them, your routines, notes and reminder settings. All of this is stored on your device, and is included in your phone's own backups if you use them. There are no accounts, and the app has no server that receives your data.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, the app reads your daily step count and sleep (time asleep, or time in bed if that's all your phone records), only to show how they line up with your pain. It does not write to Apple Health. Health data is kept on your device, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is never used for advertising or marketing. You can turn off access at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.

Notifications and motion

Reminders are scheduled on your phone by the app itself. If you allow Motion & Fitness access, the app reads your iPhone's record of walking, running, cycling, driving and recent steps, on your device only, to hold off reminders when you've been moving. It keeps only the time you last moved, and never sends this anywhere. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Motion & Fitness.

Voice notes

If you tap the record button on a check-in's notes, your iPhone asks for microphone and speech recognition access, then turns what you say into text. This is done by Apple's speech recognition, which may send the audio to Apple to be transcribed under Apple's privacy terms. The app keeps only the text, and never keeps or sends the audio itself. The button is optional, and nothing is heard until you tap it. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security.

Importing

You can bring in a backup, a file from another app, an Apple Health export or a workout written as text. The app reads these on your phone; nothing is uploaded to be read.

Sharing

The app only shares your diary when you ask it to: for example, exporting a file, or sending it to an AI chat app from the Analyse with AI screen, which tells you what's shared before you tap Share. If you share with an AI chat, the company behind it receives that health information under its own privacy terms, and what happens to it then is up to them. Analyse with AI leaves out Apple Health data. A file you export from Settings includes it, so keep that in mind if you pass that file on.

The website

The website and web version are hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, processes visitors' IP addresses to deliver pages. Anything you enter in the web version stays in your browser. The site uses no cookies or tracking.

Analytics and tracking

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. You can also delete everything from Settings › Your data, and export your diary at any time to keep a copy.

Not medical advice

Back Story helps you notice patterns. It does not diagnose or treat anything. If your pain is severe, getting worse, or comes with numbness, weakness, or changes in bladder or bowel control, see a doctor straight away.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: see the support page. Please don't post health details there, as it's public.
