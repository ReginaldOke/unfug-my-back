Privacy Policy

Unfug My Back · Last updated 30 October 2026

Unfug My Back is a diary for back pain. It is built so that your diary stays yours: it lives on your phone, and we never see it.

What the app stores

Your check-ins (pain, stress, sleep, where it hurts), the things you log alongside them, your routines, notes and reminder settings. All of this is stored only on your device. There are no accounts, and the app has no server that receives your data.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, the app reads your daily step count and sleep (time asleep, or time in bed if that's all your phone records), only to show how they line up with your pain. It does not write to Apple Health. Health data is kept on your device, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is never used for advertising or marketing. You can turn off access at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.

Notifications and motion

Reminders are scheduled on your phone by the app itself. Motion data, if used, is processed on your device to notice long periods of sitting still and is not stored or shared.

Importing

You can bring in a backup, a file from another app, an Apple Health export or a workout written as text. The app reads these on your phone; nothing is uploaded to be read.

Sharing

The app only shares your diary when you ask it to: for example, exporting a file or sending it to an AI chat app through the iOS share sheet. What happens to it then is up to the app or person you share it with.

Analytics and tracking

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. You can also delete everything from Settings › Your data, and export your diary at any time to keep a copy.

Not medical advice

Unfug My Back helps you notice patterns. It does not diagnose or treat anything. If your pain is severe, getting worse, or comes with numbness, weakness, or changes in bladder or bowel control, see a doctor straight away.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/ReginaldOke/unfug-my-back/issues.
