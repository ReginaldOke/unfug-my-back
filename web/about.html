Unfug My Back

Find out what your back is telling you.

A one-minute daily check-in that shows what eases your back pain, and what sets it off.

Try it in your browser How it works ↓

Free on iPhone. Coming soon to the App Store.

Check in

Once a day: how much it hurts, where, and what you did. About a minute.

See the pattern

Pain shown alongside sitting, walking, sleep, workouts and anything else you track.

Try a change

Clear suggestions from your own data, like cutting back on long sitting for a week.

Patterns

What moves your pain, in plain words.

After a week or two, the app points out what tends to come with more pain and what comes with less, then suggests something to try.

Check-in

Tap where it hurts.

A 3D body map, quick sliders for pain, stress and sleep, and one-tap tiles for the things you did today.

Routines and nudges

Keep moving, without thinking about it.

Mobility and gym routines with reps and weights, a gentle nudge when you've been still too long, and steps and sleep from Apple Health.

Private by design

Your diary stays on your phone.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Export it any time, or share it with an AI chat for a deeper look.

Unfug My Back helps you notice patterns. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything.
