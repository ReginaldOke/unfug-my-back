Back Story

Keep a diary of your back pain. Find out what's behind it.

Check in once a day. Back Story shows what your bad days have in common, even things that catch up with you a day or two later.

Try it in your browser How it works

Free on iPhone. Coming soon to the App Store.

  1. Check in

    How much it hurts, where, and what you did today. It takes about a minute.

  2. See the pattern

    Your pain on a chart next to sitting, walking, sleep and whatever else you track.

  3. Try a change

    Sit less for a week, say, and watch what happens to the pattern.

What your bad days have in common.

Back Story compares your days with more of something against days with less. More sitting, more driving, a poor night's sleep. You see what goes with more pain and what goes with less, side by side.

Some things hurt later.

A long day at a desk might not hurt until two days on. Sitting and driving might be fine apart but not together. Back Story looks for both, and tells you in plain words.

Tap a pattern to see the days behind it. It only calls something a pattern when it's unlikely to be chance.

Tap where it hurts.

Mark the spot on a 3D body, slide for pain, stress, sleep and how well you ate, then tap the things you did today. Add your own sliders, or hide the steps you don't need.

Your routines, getting stronger.

Keep your stretches and gym sessions in the app, with reps and weights. Time a plank or a dead hang. A chart for each move shows what's going up and what isn't.

  • Nudges that know you've moved

    Sit still too long and you get a nudge. Walk about and the next one waits, even with the app closed.

  • Apple Health

    Steps and sleep come straight in, so there's less to type.

  • A second opinion

    Export your diary any time, or share it with an AI chat for another look.

Your diary stays on your phone.

You don't need an account, and there are no ads or tracking.

Back Story doesn't diagnose or treat anything. Check with a doctor before making medical decisions.
