Log how your back feels each day, along with sleep, stress, sitting, exercise and anything else you want. Back Story charts it all and shows you the patterns.

Log your day in under a minute

Rate your pain, then stress and sleep, then tap what you did. Sitting, driving, walking, stretching, or anything you add yourself.

Steps and sleep can come in from Apple Health, and a reminder each evening helps you keep the streak going.

See what your pain follows

Back Story compares your worse days with your better ones and tells you what tends to come first. Some things show up a day or two later, and some only hurt in combination. It checks for both.

Each pattern is tested against your own history, so you only see the ones that are unlikely to be chance. Open one to see the numbers and the days behind it.

Keep track of your stretching, mobility and gym routines

Save your routines once and tick them off in your check-in. Log sets, reps and weights, and time holds like a plank or a dead hang.

Every move gets its own progress line, and your routines show up on the pain chart, so you can see if they're helping.

Get a second view from AI

Send your diary to the AI chat app you already use. It goes with instructions, so you can ask straight away what lines up with your pain and what to try next.

You choose when to share it and which app it goes to.

Your diary stays on your phone

There's no account to make, and no ads or tracking.

Back Story doesn't diagnose or treat anything. Check with a doctor before making medical decisions.
