Track your back pain and find out what affects it

Back Story turns a quick daily check-in into a clear picture of your back: how it's doing, what makes it worse and what helps.

Try it in your browser
01 · Track

Log your day in under a minute

A few taps at the end of the day covers your pain, your sleep and what you got up to. Steps and sleep come in from Apple Health, so there's less to fill in.

The more days you log, the clearer your patterns get.

02 · Patterns

Find out what's causing your pain

Back Story looks at everything you log and shows which activities go with more pain, and which go with less.

It also spots things that hurt a day or two later, like a long drive, and only flags a pattern when it's unlikely to be chance.

03 · Routines

Keep track of your stretching, mobility and gym routines

Save your routines once, then press play and follow along one move at a time, with an animated guide for each stretch. Log sets, reps and weights as you go.

Every move gets its own progress line, and your routines show up on the pain chart, so you can see if they're helping.

Your diary stays on your phone

There's no account to make, and no ads or tracking.
