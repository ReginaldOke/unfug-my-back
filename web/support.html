Common questions

Where is my data stored? Only on your phone. There are no accounts and nothing is sent to a server. See the privacy policy.

How do I back up or move my diary? Settings › Your data › Export saves a file. Import, on the same screen, brings it back on any phone.

I'm not getting reminders. Check Settings › Notifications › Unfug My Back on your iPhone, and the nudge hours in the app's Reminders. Reminders › Send a test nudge shows one in five seconds.

Steps or sleep aren't showing. Check Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Unfug My Back has Steps and Sleep turned on. Without an Apple Watch, iPhone records time in bed when a Sleep schedule is set up in the Health app.

How do I delete everything? Settings › Your data › Delete all data (press and hold), or delete the app.

Not medical advice

If your pain is severe, getting worse, or comes with numbness, weakness, or changes in bladder or bowel control, see a doctor straight away.
